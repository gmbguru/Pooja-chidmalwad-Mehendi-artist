Expert Tips

Mehendi Care & Longevity

Keep Dry First 24 Hours

Avoid water for the first 24 hours after application. This allows the mehendi to set properly and develop a darker shade.

Sunlight Exposure

Expose your mehendi to indirect sunlight for 10-15 minutes. UV rays help darken the stain naturally and enhance the color.

Warmth Helps Darken

Wrap your hands in plastic or cloth to retain body heat. Warmth helps the mehendi oxidize faster, resulting in a deeper, richer color.

Coconut Oil Massage

After 24 hours, massage with coconut oil to moisturize and protect your skin. This also helps preserve the mehendi color longer.

Lemon-Sugar Paste

Apply lemon juice mixed with sugar on dried mehendi. This helps set the color and can make it last 1-2 weeks longer.

Avoid Harsh Chemicals

Skip harsh soaps, strong detergents, and swimming pools. Use gentle, natural cleansers to preserve your mehendi for longer.
